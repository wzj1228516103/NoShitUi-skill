You don't need a green thumb

Just a little curiosity.

Your first plant? We have you covered. A few small habits make a world of difference.

How much should I water?

Check the soil first. Let the top few centimeters dry between waterings, and always let excess water drain away.

Where will my plant feel at home?

Most of these plants love bright, indirect light. Snake plants also tolerate dimmer corners. Keep leaves away from hot radiators.

What if a leaf turns yellow?

One older yellow leaf can be natural. Several yellow leaves often mean the soil is staying wet for too long. Check drainage and adjust watering.
